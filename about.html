Our story

In the beginning… still running.

Avila's Auto & Truck Repair has been family owned and operated since 1976. We specialize in general mechanic work — engines, transmissions, brake systems, suspension, exhaust, electrical and A/C — and a whole lot more.

Generations of Reno drivers have trusted us with their daily drivers, work trucks and weekend classics. We've grown with the city and kept up with the technology, from carburetors to computer diagnostics and the newest R-1234yf A/C systems — but the way we treat people hasn't changed.
